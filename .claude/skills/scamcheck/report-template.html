Positive signals

  • Valid HTTPS, no blacklist, no suspicious scripts.

What to do

  • Do not pay by bank transfer: there is no way to get the money back.
  • Look for the same product on well-known shops: a price that is too low is the strongest signal.
  • If you have already paid: contact your bank immediately, keep receipts and screenshots, report it to the police or national fraud centre.

Limitations

  • List the ➖ checks not performed and why (e.g. missing API keys, unreachable services).
  • No forms filled in, no login, no files downloaded.
